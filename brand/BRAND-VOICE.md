# Slate — Brand Voice

Words in Slate exist for one reason: to make the app easier to use. They are design material, not decoration.

---

## The voice in one line

**A good ruler doesn't compliment you on the measurement.**

Slate is calm, factual, and slightly dry. It never cheers. It never scolds. It never apologises. It states.

---

## Rules

### 1. Facts, not permission

Slate reports. It does not authorise, encourage, or forbid.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| You can still eat 1,610 calories! | `1,610 cals left` |
| Great job staying under your goal 🎉 | *(nothing)* |
| You're over budget today. | `2,610 / 2,400 cals` |
| Only 200 calories left — be careful! | `200 cals left` |

The difference between `1,610 cals left` and *"you can still eat 1,610 calories"* is the difference between an instrument and an instruction. We are an instrument.

### 2. Never celebrate a low number

No green rings for eating little. No "great day." No badges, no goal streaks, no "12 days under budget." Nothing that makes stopping *restricting* feel like failure.

The logging streak is the one counter Slate keeps, and it celebrates nothing — it states two numbers and shows dots. When it breaks, Slate says nothing at all. No "you lost it," no repair offer. The number resets and the app moves on, because the app was never keeping score of *you*, only of the writing.

This is not squeamishness. This category has a documented failure mode and encouragement feeds it directly.

### 3. Errors don't apologise

They say what happened and what to do. In the interface's voice, not a person's.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| Oops! Something went wrong 😕 | `Couldn't reach Slate. Your entries are saved and will fill in.` |
| Sorry, we couldn't understand that. | `Not sure what this is. Try adding detail?` |
| Error: barcode not found | `That barcode isn't in our database yet. Try the label scanner.` |

### 4. Empty screens are invitations

| Screen | Copy |
|---|---|
| Saved foods | `Add a nickname to a journal entry to save it.` |
| Weight chart | `No weight entries yet` |
| Journal, day 1 | `Write a food...` — and nothing else |

Never an illustration of an empty box. Never "Nothing here yet!"

### 5. Say the number, then stop

Slate's headline voice is short declaratives that end. From onboarding:

> Nutrition labels are often 20% off, so don't stress over single calories.

> Use good estimates and adjust based on progress.

Two sentences. A full stop. No third sentence explaining the first two.

### 6. Own the estimate

We tell users our numbers are approximate *before* they've typed anything, on screen five, with a link to the research. This is not a disclaimer buried in settings. It is the product's position, stated early, because it happens to be true and because it makes every later number more trustworthy, not less.

Never hedge afterwards. Having said it once, say `220 cal`, not `~220 cal`, not `about 220 cal`.

### 7. Sentence case. Active voice. Plain verbs.

`Delete my data`, not `Data Deletion`.
`Export your data`, not `Initiate Export`.
`Write a food...`, not `Enter Food Item`.

A button that says `Upgrade` produces a screen that says `Upgraded`. The vocabulary is the signposting.

### 8. No em-dashes in UI copy

They're for essays. Interface copy gets full stops.

### 9. Never explain what Slate is doing

The shimmer means it's thinking. Don't write `Analysing…`. Don't write `Powered by AI`. The user typed a food and a number appeared. That's the contract.

### 10. Never say "just"

`Just type what you ate` diminishes both the user and the work. `Type what you ate.`

## Words we use

katori · roti · chai · thali · plate · glass · cup
journal *(verb and noun)*
cals *(short, in UI)* · calories *(long, in prose)*
left · over
your kitchen

## Words we don't

wellness · journey · transform · fuel · nourish · mindful · guilt-free · cheat meal · earn · burn off · smash · crush · goals *(as a noun of aspiration)* · we've got you · let's do this

Also: **healthy**. Slate has no opinion about whether your food is healthy. It counts.

---

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Register |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | Confident, teacherly, short. It's explaining, not selling. |
| Journal | Silent. The only copy is a placeholder. |
| Errors | Flat, factual, actionable. |
| Settings | Utilitarian. Nouns and verbs. |
| Paywall | Honest and undramatic. State what Plus adds. Do not manufacture urgency. |
| App Store | Direct. Lead with what the competition gets wrong. |
| Support email | Human. A person signs it. |

## Two examples, worked

**Paywall.**

> ~~Unlock your full potential with Slate Plus! 🚀 Get unlimited access to premium features and take your health journey to the next level.~~
>
> **Slate is free forever.**
> Slate Plus adds photo logging, chat, Apple Health, custom dishes, and full history.
>
> `Monthly ₹199` · `Yearly ₹1,499`

**Over goal.**

> ~~Uh oh! You've gone over your calorie goal by 210 calories today. Tomorrow's a fresh start! 💪~~
>
> `2,610 / 2,400 cals`
> `210 cals over`

Grey. Same size as any other day. No colour change, no icon, no message.

That restraint is the entire brand. Anyone can build the text field.
