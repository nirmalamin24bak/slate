# Slate — Brand Kit

## The name

A slate is a flat grey stone you write on. Chalk on, wipe off. It carries no opinion about what you wrote. It doesn't reward you for writing daily. It doesn't judge the number.

That's the whole brand, and it's why the app is a text field.

Under **Sonal Systems Private Limited**, Vadodara.

---

## Positioning

> **A calorie journal that gets out of the way.**

Type what you ate. A number appears. That's it.

### What we stand for

**Instruments, not instructions.** We show you your BMR and your baseline. You choose your goal. We never suggest one, never nudge, never congratulate. Slate is a ruler, not a coach.

**Honest about being an estimate.** Nutrition labels are often 20% off. Restaurant oil swings a dish by 40%. We say this on the fifth screen, before you've typed anything. A number that's consistently 10% wrong still tells you the truth about your week.

**The household, not the database.** Every other app has more food entries than us. None of them know how big your katori is, or how long a litre of oil lasts your family. Four questions, asked once. That's the difference between a number and *your* number.

**Nothing between you and the line.** No submit button. No confirmation. No modal. No streak. No badge. No celebration for eating little.

**No login. No ads. No coaches. No sales calls.**

### What we are not

Not a coach. Not a nutritionist. Not a wellness platform. Not a social network. Not a habit tracker.

A notes app that knows about food.

## Typography

**Inter Tight.** One family. Every weight.

Chosen because it's a tight grotesque with a heavy display cut — it holds a headline at 40px with negative tracking and stays legible at 13px in grey. And because it has proper tabular figures, which matters more here than in almost any other app: our numbers change while you watch them.

**Tabular figures on every number, everywhere.** A calorie count that shifts its digits sideways as it resolves is a broken product.

Full scale in `spec/03-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.

## Colour

Graphite and chalk. Colour is held back for the only things that need it.

| | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#F7F7F5` | `#0E0F11` |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | `#191A1D` |
| Fill | `#EFEFEC` | `#232428` |
| Ink | `#111214` | `#F5F5F3` |
| Ink muted | `#8A8B90` | `#7E7F85` |
| Hairline | `#E3E3DF` | `#2B2C30` |

**Macros** — semantic only, never decorative:
Protein `#C4553D` clay · Carbs `#3E5C9E` indigo · Fat `#B8871F` amber

**Accent** — `#3E5C9E`. Used for the resolve shimmer, the Plus badge, focus rings. Nothing else.

Slate has no brand colour splashed across its surfaces. **The restraint is the brand.** If you find yourself reaching for the accent, you're decorating.

## Logo

A chalk mark on a stone. Not a leaf, not a flame, not an apple, not a swoosh.

- **App icon** — the wordmark's `S`, chalk-white, on `#111214`, generous margin. It should read at 40px on a crowded home screen.
- **Wordmark** — `Slate` set in Inter Tight 700, tracking `-0.03em`, lowercase-height `l` and `t` ascenders kept short.
- **Never** place the logo on a photograph. Never add a gradient. Never animate it.

Icons throughout the app are placeholders for now and will be redrawn. Line weight 1.5px, 24×24 grid, rounded caps.

## Photography & imagery

Almost none. Slate is a text field.

Where an image is unavoidable (App Store screenshots), photograph real food on a real table in real Indian daylight — a steel thali, a chipped katori, a stack of rotis under a cloth. Never a styled bowl of grain on marble. Never a woman laughing at a salad.

---

## Motion

Three animations, and no more:

1. **Resolve shimmer** — a barely-there gradient sweep under a line that's thinking
2. **Number settle** — the calorie fades and rises 4px when it lands
3. **Sheet spring** — platform default

No confetti. No haptics on logging food. Logging food is not an achievement.
