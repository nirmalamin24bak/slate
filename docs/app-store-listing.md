## Description

> **No login. No coaches. No sales calls.**
>
> Slate is a notes app that knows about food. Type what you ate on a line. A number appears next to it. That is the whole app.
>
> It is built for Indian food: roti, dal, sabzi, chai, biryani, by the katori and the plate. Numbers come from the Indian Food Composition Tables, computed on our servers, not guessed.
>
> No login. No ads. No data sold. Your entries stay in India, and you can export or delete all of them from Settings.
>
> Slate is free. The complete journal, macros, your logging streak, export, and 30 days of history cost nothing, forever.
>
> Slate Plus adds photo logging, chat, Apple Health, custom dishes, stats, and full history. ₹199 a month or ₹1,499 a year. Refunds go through Apple; we never hold your money.
>
> Slate is for adults. It is rated 18+ and does not collect data from anyone under 18.

## What Slate is (for the founders' reference)

One paragraph, one idea: a notes app that knows about food. Type a line, a number appears. Slate is not a coach, not a nutritionist, not a plan. It counts, and it stops there. Any listing revision that drifts toward advice or aspiration is off-brand; check every draft against `brand/BRAND-VOICE.md` before submitting.

## What Plus adds

Stated flat, no urgency, matching the paywall register: photo logging, chat, Apple Health, custom dishes, stats, fiber and sugar, kitchen calibration, saved foods, widgets, full history. No countdowns, no "limited offer", no locked-feature nagging in the listing.

## Age rating

18+. Slate collects age at onboarding and does not proceed with any calculation for users under 18 (`spec/08-PRIVACY-DPDP.md`, children section). Set the App Store age rating to match.

## Keywords

Apple keyword field, 100 characters, comma-separated, no spaces after commas:

```
calorie journal,calorie counter,indian food,roti,dal,food diary,IFCT,food log,macro tracker,chai
```

Banned words from `brand/BRAND-VOICE.md` apply here too: no wellness, no journey, no transform, no fuel, no nourish, no healthy, no guilt-free. If a keyword needs one of those to rank, we do not want that ranking.
